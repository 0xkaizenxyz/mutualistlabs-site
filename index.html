Agent 01 · LedgerLens

Ask what a wallet received. Every claim cited.

A read-only agent. Give it a public address and a question, and it explains the incoming transfers: which tokens, from whom, when, their fair value at receipt, and anything that looks like spam or address poisoning.

+1,250.00 USDCtx 0x8f3a…c21e
+0.42 ETHtx 0x1b7d…09fa
⚠ lookalike token, ignoretx 0xc40e…77b2

Illustration. Real answers print full hashes and block timestamps.

What it does

  • Incoming transfers on Base, Ethereum mainnet and Arbitrum One
  • Senders, with labels only where a curated label exists
  • Fair value at receipt from a named price source
  • Spam, dust and lookalike-token warnings
  • A clear "Data gaps" line whenever coverage is incomplete

What it won't do

  • Hold keys, sign anything or move funds. Never send it a seed phrase.
  • Give tax, legal or investment advice.
  • Answer questions outside wallet receipts.

Use it

LedgerLens is an AI agent on AntSeed. Automatic routing only picks sellers with a trust score of 60 or more, which currently takes a staked pool; LedgerLens is unstaked, so routing won't choose it. Pin it by its peer ID. All three ways below go to the same seller. You pay per request in USDC from your AntSeed balance: a typical answer costs about 0.5 to 2 US cents, a busy wallet up to about 4 cents, and an off-topic decline under a cent. Prices are $0.50 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output tokens.

First time? Run antseed buyer deposit for your funding address and send USDC on Base (about 2 USDC is plenty). A running buyer moves it into your deposits for a 0.05 USDC fee. Your first request to a seller holds up to 1 USDC of that in a payment channel; you pay only for what you use, and the rest is released when the channel closes.

From any tool, via the local buyer. Put the peer ID in front of the service name:

antseed buyer start
curl http://localhost:8377/v1/messages \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"model":"32d752272593c042888bb6e997c466d3574a9f52@ledgerlens",
       "max_tokens":2048,
       "messages":[{"role":"user","content":
       "What did 0x… receive on Base in the last 30 days?"}]}'

Use the Anthropic Messages endpoint (/v1/messages); the answer comes back as a stream of Server-Sent Events.

Pin it for every request (until you clear it), then send plain "model":"ledgerlens":

antseed buyer connection set --peer 32d752272593c042888bb6e997c466d3574a9f52
antseed buyer connection clear    # to undo

In the AntSeed desktop app (AI VPN): pick LedgerLens as the seller for the ledgerlens service, then ask your question in chat.

Ask one wallet, one chain (Base, Ethereum or Arbitrum One) and a period of up to 90 days at a time. Longer periods are covered in at most two 90-day parts (180 days); the answer says which dates it covered, so ask again for anything earlier. NFTs, outgoing transfers and balances are not covered.

In the desktop app, addresses in copied answers can show as [address hidden]. That comes from the app (AntSeed issue #1506), not LedgerLens; the answers themselves contain every address in full.
