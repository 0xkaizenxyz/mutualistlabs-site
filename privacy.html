Effective 2026-11-06. Operator: Mutualist Labs (mutualistlabs.com, GitHub 0xkaizenxyz, email 0xkaizenxyz@gmail.com). LedgerLens is an AntSeed seller (peer 32d752272593c042888bb6e997c466d3574a9f52, service ledgerlens).

This notice says what LedgerLens stores when you send it a request, how long, and who else sees it. Published at mutualistlabs.com/privacy.html; this file in the LedgerLens repository is the source.

What you send

A request carries your messages (usually a wallet address, chain, period and question), the system prompt of the app you use, and AntSeed routing headers that include your buyer peer ID. Wallet addresses and transactions are public on-chain data. Don't send anything you want kept private: no seed phrases, private keys, names, emails or account numbers. LedgerLens never needs them and refuses keys and seed phrases. If you paste a password, access token or API key anyway (for example after password= or as email / password), LedgerLens replaces it with [removed by LedgerLens] before your message reaches the language model, and tells you to change it.

We do not store the full text of your conversation, the model's answers, your IP address, or your payment wallet beyond what the AntSeed node records for payment channels.

Logs live on one server (a DigitalOcean droplet in its San Francisco region, sfo2), readable only by the operator. They are used to run and fix the service, measure demand (which questions people ask, how often tools fail) and check billing. We don't sell them, share them, or use them to train models.

Who else processes your request

  • Anthropic runs the language model. Your messages and the tool results go to the Anthropic API under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, which state that "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content" and that data is processed under Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum. How long Anthropic keeps API data is set by Anthropic's own policies, not by us.
  • Answers are generated by an AI model. Check anything important against a block explorer before relying on it.
  • dRPC and public RPC endpoints receive the wallet addresses and blocks we query. Coinbase and other public price sources receive token symbols and times, not your address.
  • AntSeed network peers see the encrypted connection and payment-channel activity, not your messages.
  • Discord carries the operator's private alert channel. For each request it receives a shortened form of your buyer peer ID (first 6 and last 4 characters), the service, token counts, estimated cost, latency, status, up to 200 characters of any error message, and a note when the request was declined as out of scope or tried to override LedgerLens's instructions or tool choice; alerts about open payment channels can include buyer peer IDs. Never your messages or the model's answers. Discord keeps these messages under its own policies.

Your choices

  • Ask about any address: LedgerLens doesn't know who owns the addresses you ask about, and neither do we. Your own buyer peer ID is recorded with each request (see the table), so for 30 days the logs link it to the addresses you asked about.
  • To have log lines from your requests deleted before 30 days, send your buyer peer ID and approximate request times to 0xkaizenxyz@gmail.com (or 0xkaizenxyz on GitHub). We can delete matching lines from the request and tool logs. AntSeed's metering and payment records can't be removed by us while they are needed for settlement.
