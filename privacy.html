Effective 2026-11-09. Operator: Mutualist Labs, which is not a registered company but the name under which an individual in the United States runs LedgerLens (mutualistlabs.com, GitHub 0xkaizenxyz, email 0xkaizenxyz@gmail.com). This notice says what LedgerLens stores when you send it a request, how long, and who else sees it. Published at mutualistlabs.com/privacy.html; this file in the LedgerLens repository is the source.

What you send

A request carries your messages (usually a wallet address, chain, period and question), the system prompt of the app you use, and AntSeed routing headers that include your buyer peer ID. Wallet addresses and transactions are public on-chain data. Don't send anything you want kept private: no seed phrases, private keys, names, emails or account numbers. LedgerLens never needs them and refuses keys and seed phrases. If you paste a password, access token or API key anyway (for example after password= or as email / password), LedgerLens replaces it with [removed by LedgerLens] before your message reaches the language model, and tells you to change it.

We do not store the full text of your conversation, the model's answers, your IP address, or your payment wallet beyond what the AntSeed node records for payment channels.

Logs live on one cloud server hosted by DigitalOcean, readable only by the operator. They are used to run and fix the service, measure demand (which questions people ask, how often tools fail) and check billing. We don't sell them, share them, or use them to train models.

Who else processes your request

  • Anthropic runs the language model. Your messages and the tool results go to the Anthropic API under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, which state that "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content" and that data is processed under Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum. Our Anthropic organization uses Anthropic's standard data retention: Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except content its automated safety systems flag (kept for up to 2 years) or where the law requires otherwise. Anthropic does not use this data to train models without permission. We have not arranged zero data retention.
  • Answers are generated by an AI model. Check anything important against a block explorer before relying on it.
  • dRPC receives the wallet addresses and blocks we query. When dRPC fails, the same queries go to a backup: Tenderly (all three chains), MEV Blocker (Ethereum) or the Arbitrum public endpoint, and their answers are checked against a second source. Coinbase and other public price sources receive token symbols and times, not your address.
  • AntSeed network peers see the encrypted connection and payment-channel activity, not your messages.
  • Discord carries the operator's private alert channel. For each request it receives the service, token counts, estimated cost, latency, status, up to 200 characters of any error message, and a note when the request was declined as out of scope or tried to override LedgerLens's instructions or tool choice; alerts about open payment channels include the channel ID, which is public on Base. Never your buyer peer ID, your messages or the model's answers. Discord keeps these messages under its own policies.

Your choices

  • Ask about any address: LedgerLens doesn't know who owns the addresses you ask about, and neither do we. Your own buyer peer ID is recorded with each request (see the table), so for 30 days the logs link it to the addresses you asked about.
  • To have log lines from your requests deleted before 30 days, send your buyer peer ID and approximate request times to 0xkaizenxyz@gmail.com (or 0xkaizenxyz on GitHub). We can delete matching lines from the request and tool logs. AntSeed's metering and payment records can't be removed by us while they are needed for settlement.

Who is responsible, and your rights under the GDPR

The operator named at the top decides why and how this data is processed and is the controller under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which we treat as applying to requests from people in the EU, the EEA and the UK. We have not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK. Contact: 0xkaizenxyz@gmail.com.

Why we process it (legal basis):

  • Answering your request and billing it (contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): your messages, the tool calls, and the AntSeed metering and payment records.
  • Running, securing and improving the service (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)): the request, tool and seller logs, the weekly report and the operator alerts. Our interest is keeping the service working, finding errors and abuse, and learning what people ask. We keep these records for 30 days or less and don't use them to find out who you are.

Your data is processed in the United States, where our server, Anthropic and our alert channel are, and by the other providers listed above wherever they operate. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you is made automatically.

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its use, or to receive it in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Send the request and your buyer peer ID (with approximate request times) to 0xkaizenxyz@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.
